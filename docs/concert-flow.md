# From "let's do a concert" to the stage

How a set moves through Shajara. Walked through with Laden 2 (Ghorab · Marwan, Amr, Sherif).

## 1. We decide to play
Whoever runs the night: **Sets → New set** — name, date, venue, cast. Cast = people of the space, people **from your other spaces** (same person, their sounds come along), or a name for someone who never logs in.

## 2. Building the set (one person, before we meet)
- **Add a track**: a version of an existing one (choose who takes whose part) or a new one. The original is never touched.
- Per section in the set's version: **who plays what, on which sound** · **cues** (who gives it, or "production") · a note.
- **Send-out link** → one link for everyone, no login.

## 3. The meeting (we sit together) — Review mode
- Section by section, one card each: the cue into it, everyone's part and sound, every recording (▶), the notes. Tap a card = "we're here".
- One phone edits the set ("Change this section in Shajara ↗" lands on it); everyone else taps **↻** to catch up.

## 4. At home (Study)
- Pick yourself. Per song: **your parts, one card each** — the section, what you play and on which sound, how you come in, the line to learn (▶, repeat, slow), what's around you, notes. Tick **I know this** as you go; "Play my lines in order" runs through the song.
- **My night** = your whole night in order: entries, sound changes, what you wait for.
- No recording of your line yet? **Record it in Shajara ↗** on the card opens the app on that section (switches to the right space, asks for its passcode once). Same link to leave a note.
- The arrangement board is one tap away ("Show the board") but not the point here.

## 5. Rehearsal — one track at a time
Everyone has the link open on their phone; one person has the set open in Shajara to write things down.

1. **Read it together, instruments down** · *Review mode, Everyone.* Go card by card: section name, who plays what, the cue into it. Tap the card = "we're here". Play the recorded lines (▶) so everyone hears the same thing. Questions get settled here, not with instruments in hand.
2. **Fix what's wrong on the spot** · the person with Shajara edits the set's version (line-up, cue, sound, a note). Everyone taps ↻.
3. **Run it section by section** · *Rehearse mode, I am me.* Board + your part. Tap the section you're on. Stop, fix, repeat the section until it sits.
4. **Run the whole track** · stay in Rehearse, or try Stage mode to see if the checklist is enough for you on the night.
5. **Before moving on: tasks and notes** · open the Tasks box: "I'll fix the pad levels" → who. Lines that still need a recording → note it on the section ("Open this section in Shajara" from the card) or record it right there.

Next track. At the end, the open tasks are the homework; My night is what everyone studies before the next one.

## 6. Stage (Stage mode)
- Only your checklist per song: **WATCH / LISTEN** + what to do, sound set-up and switches in between. Prev/next song, swipe works.
- Opens without signal (last copy kept on the phone; ▶ needs a connection).

## Honest gaps
- Section lengths are relative until someone fills in bar counts.
- Nobody is notified of changes; the page reloads itself when you come back to it after 15 min (not on stage), otherwise ↻.
- Recording into a set means opening that space (its passcode) and picking yourself there.
